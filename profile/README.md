Petonexus builds native VR experiences, practical modding tools, and reversible installation workflows. Our current focus is bringing **The Legend of Heroes: Trails of Cold Steel** to VR and making PC game modding easier to understand, install, verify, and undo.

## Featured projects

### Trails of Cold Steel VR

A fan-made native OpenXR VR mod for the PC version of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails of Cold Steel*.

- Native stereo rendering and 6DoF head tracking
- Tracked VR hands and first-person exploration
- VR-adapted exploration and battle interfaces
- Original third-person battle cinematography
- OpenXR support, with Quest 3, Virtual Desktop, and VDXR as the current development target

[Visit the website](https://www.trailsvr.pro) · [Watch the early alpha preview](https://youtu.be/IXdkOXGHeAE) · [Follow releases](https://github.com/petonexus/tocs-vr-releases)

> The project is in active development. No public build is available yet.

### Moddin Desktop

A Windows-first mod manager for VR mods, performance tools, overlays, and game tweaks. Moddin detects installed games, previews every change, keeps backups, verifies installations, and can undo what it applied.

Its data-driven capabilities already cover workflows around UEVR, OpenXR helpers, OBS VR capture, OptiScaler, ReShade, and other game-specific tools.

[Explore Moddin Desktop](https://github.com/petonexus/moddin-desktop) · [Contribute a capability](https://github.com/petonexus/moddin-community-capabilities)

## How we build

- **VR-native interaction:** head tracking, tracked hands, stereo rendering, and interfaces designed for a headset.
- **Reversible modding:** changes should be previewable, verifiable, backed up, and removable.
- **Clear provenance:** original work, upstream projects, third-party components, and licenses stay identifiable.
- **Useful releases:** public builds should include checksums, installation instructions, release notes, and known issues.
