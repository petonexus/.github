## Featured projects

### Trails of Cold Steel VR

A free, fan-made OpenXR mod for the Steam version of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails of Cold Steel* (PC v1.6, English). Tested with Meta Quest 3 and Virtual Desktop.

- Explore in stereo first person with head tracking, a visible body, and hands tracked through Touch controllers.
- Play turn-based battles with the original third-person camera, an adjustable tabletop miniature, or experimental first person.
- Use optional palm-mounted battle commands, a wrist minimap, and floating panels for dialogue and results.
- Adjust comfort settings for seated or standing play, including snap or smooth turning.

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/petonexus/tocs-vr-releases/releases/latest) · [Installation guide](https://www.trailsvr.pro/guide/) · [Watch gameplay](https://youtu.be/BCXjr3M0slI) · [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/thelegendofheroestrailsofcoldsteel/mods/6)

### Gymmons

An original workout game for Android and iPhone. Training days earn XP, help hatch GymOvos, and evolve the Gymmons in your collection.

- Log activities, duration, notes, and photos. Import workouts from Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone.
- Collect and evolve creatures with original art, track them in your Gymdex, and build an active team.
- Track your consistency with a workout calendar and streaks. Invite friends to private leagues with weekly rankings and automatic battles.

[Visit the Gymmons website](https://gymmons.app/app) · [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.petonexus.gymmonsfront) · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/gymmons/id6798862893)

## Tools and supporting projects

### Cold Steel DLSS

A free mod for playing *Trails of Cold Steel* on a regular monitor. It applies NVIDIA DLAA to the 3D scene at your selected resolution and leaves the game's menus and HUD untouched. The public pre-release requires an NVIDIA RTX card and PC game version 1.6.

[Downloads and installation](https://github.com/petonexus/cold-steel-dlss-releases) · [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/thelegendofheroestrailsofcoldsteel/mods/7)

### Moddin Desktop

A Windows 10/11 x64 mod manager in public beta. It detects installed Steam and Epic games and previews, backs up, verifies, and rolls back changes made through its recipes. Available recipes vary by game and cover tools such as UEVR, OBS VR Capture, OptiScaler, and ReShade.

[Download the beta](https://github.com/petonexus/moddin-desktop/releases) · [User guide](https://github.com/petonexus/moddin-desktop/blob/main/docs/USER-GUIDE.md)

### Moddin Community Capabilities

A community catalog of YAML recipes that Moddin Desktop can install. A recipe declares how to check, install, verify, and uninstall a mod. Contributors can add support for a mod without changing the desktop app.

[Browse the catalog](https://github.com/petonexus/moddin-community-capabilities) · [Submit a recipe](https://github.com/petonexus/moddin-community-capabilities/blob/main/SUBMITTING.md)

## How we build

- **VR-native interaction:** head tracking, tracked hands, stereo rendering, and interfaces designed for a headset.
- **Reversible modding:** changes should be previewable, verifiable, backed up, and removable.
- **Clear provenance:** original work, upstream projects, third-party components, and licenses stay identifiable.
- **Useful releases:** public builds should include checksums, installation instructions, release notes, and known issues.
